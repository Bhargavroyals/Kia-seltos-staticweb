Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist

When activated, Standard Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist w/ Pedestrian and Cyclist Detection uses sensors and a camera to scan the road ahead for potential hazards, and can even help automatically apply the brakes when the system detects a potential obstruction in your path. Additionally, available Junction Turning Assist is designed to help detect oncoming vehicles when turning left through an intersection

Blind-Spot Collision Warning

If an object is detected in your blind spot, the available and activated system sends out visual and/or auditory signals to help warn you of it.

Rear Cross-Traffic Collision-Avoidance Assist

If oncoming obstacles are detected while backing up, the available Rear Cross-Traffic Collision-Avoidance Assist can help alert the driver or apply the brakes to help avoid a potential collision.

Navigation-based Smart Cruise Control w/ Stop & Go

When activated, Navigation-based Smart Cruise Control w/ Stop & Go can help maintain a predetermined following distance from the vehicle detected ahead, adjusting driving speed, and can even bring the vehicle to a complete stop and start again within 3 seconds of stopping. Navigation-based information is also designed to adapt to changing speed limits

Highway Driving Assist

The available, hands-on, Highway Driving Assist when activated can help to maintain a comfortable speed and distance from the vehicle detected ahead of you using a forward-facing camera, radar sensors, and navigation data
